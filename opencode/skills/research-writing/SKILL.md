# Research Writing

- [[research-writing/synthesis/synthesis|synthesis]] — combine evidence-linked sources.
- [[research-writing/general-paper/general-paper|general paper]] — write a formal academic paper.
- [[research-writing/ml-paper/ml-paper|ML paper]] — write a NeurIPS, ICML, or ICLR paper.
- [[research-writing/project-workflow/project-workflow|project workflow]] — reconcile research-project planning state.

For any paper whose content is mathematical — proofs, definitions, theorem
blocks, mathematical notation — load
[[mathematics/writing/policy/policy|mathematical-writing-policy]] alongside
the subskill above and write the prose under its single principle: state the
mathematics at its highest natural structural level (named ambient category,
typed morphisms, complete exact/(co)fiber sequences, diagrams where the fact
is diagrammatic), never an English paraphrase of it.
